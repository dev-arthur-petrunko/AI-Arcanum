♌ The fifth sign, element Fire, ruled by the Sun (Jul 23 – Aug 22). Leo is creativity, dignity and generosity of heart. It shines at the centre of the stage, loves recognition and protects its people with royal pomp. Strengths: charisma, loyalty, creativity. Vulnerabilities: pride, craving praise, drama.
