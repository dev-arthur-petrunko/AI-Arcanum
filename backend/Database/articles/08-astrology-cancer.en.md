♋ The fourth sign, element Water, ruled by the Moon (Jun 21 – Jul 22). Cancer is the sign of home, memory and emotional depth. It senses the world through attachments and protects those it loves with a mother's intuition. Strengths: care, empathy, devotion. Vulnerabilities: vulnerability to hurt, over-protection, retreating into a shell under stress.
