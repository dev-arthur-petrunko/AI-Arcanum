♒ The eleventh sign, element Air, co-ruled by Saturn and Uranus (Jan 20 – Feb 18). Aquarius is the sign of the future: independence, originality, friendship with ideas. It views the world from aside and sees what it could become. Strengths: humanism, intellect, freedom. Vulnerabilities: detachment, needless rebellion, emotional coolness.
