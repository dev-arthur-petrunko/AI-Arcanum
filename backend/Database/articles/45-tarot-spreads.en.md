A spread gives structure to a question: each position represents a different aspect of the situation. Below are the main layouts, from simple to classic.

**Daily card (1 card).** A quick check-in — "What awaits me today?" One card sets the tone.

**Yes / No (1 card).** A concise answer to a direct question. Major card upright = yes; reversed or minor = no.

**Three cards (3).** The minimal classic: Past – Present – Future. A quick snapshot.

**Situation – Action – Outcome (3).** An alternative to three cards: what is happening, what to do, where it leads.

**Choice A or B (5).** Two paths, intermediate steps, and the outcome of each option.

**About a relationship (6).** Foundation, needs, dynamics, obstacles, advice, and outcome.

**About love (7).** A deep look at a love bond: you, partner, connection, obstacles, context, and advice.

**Month ahead (5).** Overview of the current week, coming days, mid-month, monthly outcome, advice.

**Horseshoe (7).** A classic week overview: past, present, future, home, hopes, strengths, advice.

**Celtic cross (10).** The full picture: situation, past, future, potential, recent past, near future, environment, hopes/fears, outcome.

**Year ahead (12).** A year forecast by months or themes: what each period brings.
