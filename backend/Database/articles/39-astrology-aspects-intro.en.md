✦ Aspects are the angles between planets in a chart, describing their dialogue. The main ones: conjunction (0°), sextile (60°), square (90°), trine (120°) and opposition (180°). Harmonious aspects (sextile, trine) stream support; tense ones (square, opposition) fuel growth through difficulty.
