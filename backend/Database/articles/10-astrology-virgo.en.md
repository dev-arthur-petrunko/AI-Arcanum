♍ The sixth sign, element Earth, ruled by Mercury (Aug 23 – Sep 22). Virgo brings order to the world: precision, analysis, service. It sees details others miss and perfects things. Strengths: practicality, reliability, intellect. Vulnerabilities: perfectionism, self-criticism, anxiety over outcomes.
