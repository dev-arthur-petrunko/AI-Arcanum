♈ The first sign of the zodiac, element Fire, ruled by Mars (Mar 21 – Apr 19). Aries opens the yearly cycle: initiative, courage, directness and the skill of starting. Its energy is impulsive and competitive — a warrior who loves to be first. Strengths: decisiveness, independence, drive. Vulnerabilities: impatience, temper, quitting halfway once the thrill fades.
