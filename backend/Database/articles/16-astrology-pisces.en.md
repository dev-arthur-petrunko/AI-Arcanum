♓ The twelfth sign, element Water, co-ruled by Jupiter and Neptune (Feb 19 – Mar 20). Pisces closes the cycle: empathy, imagination, connection with the unseen. It dissolves into feelings and art, keenly sensing the world's moods. Strengths: compassion, intuition, creativity. Vulnerabilities: boundlessness, escapism, self-sacrifice.
