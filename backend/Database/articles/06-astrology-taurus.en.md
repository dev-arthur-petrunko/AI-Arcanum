♉ The second sign, element Earth, ruled by Venus (Apr 20 – May 20). Taurus values stability, comfort and the sensual beauty of the world. A builder and keeper: patient, reliable, generous to its own. Strengths: patience, practicality, loyalty. Vulnerabilities: stubbornness, resistance to change, attachment to comfort and possession.
