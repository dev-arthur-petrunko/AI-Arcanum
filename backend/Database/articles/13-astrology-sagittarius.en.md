♐ The ninth sign, element Fire, ruled by Jupiter (Nov 22 – Dec 21). Sagittarius is the philosopher-traveller: seeking meaning, horizons, faith in the best. It aims high and expands borders — of knowledge, places and the dream of freedom. Strengths: optimism, honesty, breadth. Vulnerabilities: tactlessness, instability, promises without details.
